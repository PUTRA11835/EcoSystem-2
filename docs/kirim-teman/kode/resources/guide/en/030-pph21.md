The PPh 21 page holds the tax tables that [[payroll]] uses to withhold employee income tax, and a report of the tax withheld per payroll period.

## Who uses this

Finance and tax staff. The page has two tabs: `Settings` and `Report`. You only see the tabs your role allows.

## Before you start

- Employees need a tax status (PTKP) in `Master` › `Employee`, and a tax ID where available, so that the right tax rules apply.
- Tax tables are kept per tax year. Make sure the year you need exists.

## Step by step

### Review or change the tax tables (Settings)

1. Open `Commercial & Finance` › `PPh 21` › `Settings`.
2. Choose the `Tax year`.
3. Open the sub-tab you need: `PTKP`, `Progressive Rates` or `TER Rates`.
4. Edit the values and click `Save PTKP`, `Save rates` or `Save category A` (B or C for the other TER categories).
5. Under `Calculation settings`, review the `Occupational cost rate (%)`, the `Occupational cost maximum per month (IDR)` and whether employer-paid BPJS premiums count as gross income. Click `Save settings`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Payroll reads every rate from this page. Compare the tables with the official regulation before payroll runs; a wrong rate gives wrong tax on every payslip. The default for employer-paid premiums follows the regulation, so change it only on Accounting's decision.

### Add a new tax year

1. In `New year (copy of ...)`, enter the year.
2. Click `Create year`. The new year starts as a copy of the current one.
3. Adjust the tables where the rules changed.

A year cannot be deleted. When a year is already used by a finished payroll, the page shows a notice and the values can no longer be changed.

### Review withheld tax (Report)

1. Open the `Report` tab.
2. Choose the `Payroll period`.
3. Read the totals: `Employees`, `Gross income for tax` and `Total PPh 21`.
4. Check the table per employee (`PTKP / Tax ID`, `Gross`, `PPh 21`, `Method`). Click `Export Excel` to download it.

## What happens next

Each calculated payroll period applies the tables of its tax year. The final tax month of the year is adjusted by the progressive rates; the setting for it is in [[payroll]] `Settings`.

## Common questions

**Why are the buttons missing?** You may have view-only access, or the year is already used by a finished payroll.

**Where do I switch PPh 21 off for one person?** In the payroll period, on the employee's row (see [[payroll]]).
