The `SLA` menu measures how fast tickets are answered and resolved compared with the targets agreed with each customer. `SLA Report` shows the results; `SLA Config` holds the policies.

## Who uses this

Delivery managers and heads read `SLA Report`. Administrators or people with the right access maintain policies in `SLA Config`, which appears only for them.

## Before you start

SLA applies to tickets of type `Incident`, `Change Request` and `Consult`. A policy is matched by the ticket's delivery support, `Priority` and `Scale`, so set the `Scale` when you create or validate a ticket. See [[tickets]] and [[ticket-validation]].

## Step by step

### Read the SLA Report

1. Open `Work & Service` › `SLA` › `SLA Report`. The page is titled `SLA Report` and refreshes every 60 seconds.
2. Narrow the list with the filters for customer, date (`From` and `To`, then `Apply`), `Status`, type of service and ticket type. Use `Clear Filter` to start over.
3. Read the columns in three groups: ticket information, `SLA Response` and `SLA Resolution`. They include the date received, the due date and time, the date responded, the durations and the SLA status.
4. Click the `Log / PDF` action on a row to open the `SLA Log` of that ticket. There you can see the response and resolution figures, the total waiting time and the `Ball Holder`, and use `Refresh` or `Download PDF`.
5. Click `Export SLA` to download the report as an Excel file. The report shows up to 200 records at a time.

### Manage policies (SLA Config)

1. Open `Work & Service` › `SLA` › `SLA Config`.
2. Click `Add Policy`.
3. Select the `Delivery Support`, then the `Priority` (`1: Very High` to `4: Low`) and the `Scale` (`Simple`, `Medium`, `Complex`).
4. Enter `Response (Hour)` and `Resolution (Hour)`.
5. Either tick `24/7 Calendar Hours` or enter `Work Start`, `Work End`, `Break Start` and `Break End` so only business hours are counted.
6. Save the policy. To change it later, use the edit action; switch `Policy Active` off to stop applying it to new tickets. Deleting asks for confirmation with `Yes, Delete`.

## How the clock works

- The clock runs while the ball is with the helpdesk, in `Open` and `Inprocess`.
- It stops while the ticket is `Waiting on Customer`, `Waiting on 3rd Party`, `Waiting to Confirmation` or on `Hold`. The `Ball Holder` shows who the ticket is waiting on.
- The SLA is finalised when the ticket becomes `Closed` or `Cancelled`. The result is shown as met or breached against the policy hours.

## What happens next

Each ticket shows its SLA figures in the report as soon as it has a matching policy. Tickets without a matching policy cannot be judged until one is added.

## Common questions

**I do not see `SLA Config`.** Only people with that permission can manage policies.

**Why is a ticket's duration shorter than the calendar time?** Waiting time and non-working hours are not counted.
