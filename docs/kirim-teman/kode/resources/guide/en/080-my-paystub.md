`Paystub` lists the payslips that Finance has published for you. You can download each one as a PDF.

## Who uses this

Every employee who has the `Paystub` menu. You see only your own payslips.

## Before you start

A payslip appears only after Finance publishes it for the period. Until then the page says `No payslip has been published for you yet.`

## Step by step

### Read your payslips

1. Open `Paystub` from the sidebar.
2. Find the period in the first column. It shows the date range and when it was published.
3. Read `Slip no.`, `Earnings`, `Deductions` and `Take-home pay`.

### Download a payslip

1. In the row of the period you need, go to the `Download` column.
2. Select `Indonesian` or `English` to choose the language of the PDF.
3. Save the file when your browser asks.

The most recent periods are listed first.

### See a short recap in My Profile

`My Profile` › `Basic Payment` shows a read-only recap titled `Recent Paystubs`. Full history and PDF downloads stay on the `Paystub` page.

## What happens next

Nothing further is required. If a figure looks wrong, contact HR or Finance and mention the period and the slip number.

## Common questions

**My payslip is not listed.** The period may not be published yet, or you may not be part of that payroll run. Ask Finance.

**The page says paystubs are not available for my role in My Profile.** Your role has no `Paystub` menu. See [[access-and-menus]].

> [!NOTE]
> A payslip contains personal salary data. Download it only on devices you trust and do not share the file.
