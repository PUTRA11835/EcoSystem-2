This topic shows the main parts of the screen and how to personalise them, so you can reach any page quickly.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in to EcoSystem. What you see in the sidebar depends on your access (see [[access-and-menus]]).

## The screen at a glance

- **Sidebar** on the left: all the menus you can use.
- **Top bar**: the menu button, the page title, the sound and bell icons, and the menu under your name.
- **Page area**: the page you opened.

On a phone or a narrow window the sidebar is hidden. Select the menu button at the top left to open it.

## Dashboard

`Dashboard` is the first item in the sidebar and the page you land on after signing in. It greets you by name. If you can use `My Attendance`, the greeting card also has `Check-in` and `Check-out` buttons. Below it you may see:

- `Company overview` and other summary cards, shown only for the areas you have access to.
- A shortcut panel with two tabs, `Favorite` and `All Menu`.
- Your attendance summary for the month.

## Step by step

### Find a menu

1. Click the `Search menu...` box at the top of the sidebar.
2. Type part of a menu name. The sidebar shows only the matching menus.
3. Select the menu, or clear the search with the `x` button.

### Fold the sidebar

1. Select a section title, for example `My Workspace`, to fold or unfold it. EcoSystem remembers your choice in this browser.
2. Select the double-arrow button next to the search box to collapse or expand all sections at once.
3. Select `Collapse to icons` at the bottom of the sidebar to shrink it to an icon strip. Hover over or select a section icon to see its menus. Select `Expand sidebar` to return.

### Pin your favorite menus

1. Point at a menu in the sidebar. A pin icon appears.
2. Select the pin (`Pin to sidebar`). Select it again to remove it (`Unpin from sidebar`).
3. Open `Dashboard` and choose the `Favorite` tab to reach your pinned menus in one click. `All Menu` lists every menu you can open.

> [!NOTE]
> You can pin up to 6 menus. If the limit is reached, remove one first.

### Check notifications

1. Select the bell icon in the top bar. A red number shows how many notifications are unread.
2. Select a notification to open the related page.
3. Select `Mark all read` to clear the badge, or `View all` to open the `Notifications` page.
4. On the `Notifications` page, use the `All`, `Unread` and `Read` tabs and the search box to find older items.

The speaker icon next to the bell turns the notification sound on or off.

### Use the menu under your name

Select your name in the top-right corner to open `My Profile`, `Settings`, `User Guide` or `Sign Out`.

### Personalise your Settings

1. Open `Settings` from the sidebar or from the menu under your name.
2. Choose a tab on the left:
   - `Appearance`: `Theme` (Light, Dark or Auto), `Accent color`, `Sidebar style` (Gradient or Solid), `Font size`, `Compact mode` and `Show animations`.
   - `Preferences`: `Auto-save`, `Auto-refresh` and `Keyboard shortcuts`.
   - `Notifications`: switch notifications, email and push alerts on or off, and pick the notification sounds.
   - `Security`: change your password. Enter a new password of at least 8 characters, confirm it and select `Update password`.
   - `Language & Region`: `Language` (English or Bahasa Indonesia), `Timezone` and `Date format`.
3. Select `Save changes`. Select `Reset` on the `Appearance` tab to return to the defaults.

> [!TIP]
> Notification sounds are stored in your browser, so you set them again on each new browser or computer.

## Common questions

**A menu I need is not in the sidebar.** Your role may not include it. See [[access-and-menus]].

**The sidebar is too wide or too busy.** Use `Collapse to icons`, fold sections you rarely use, or pin your favorites.
