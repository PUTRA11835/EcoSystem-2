`Home` is the first page you see after you sign in. It gives you shortcuts to what you use most and a quick look at the numbers around you.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in. What you see depends on your role, so some cards or shortcuts may differ for you.

## Before you start

The numbers on `Home` show only what your role may see. If a number looks low, it is not a fault: other data is outside your access.

## Step by step

![The Home dashboard with the greeting, Command Center, cards and ticket overview](images/guide/home-dashboard.png)

*Screenshot with sample data.*

1. Read the greeting at the top. It shows your name, your role, the date and the time.
2. Use `Command Center` for shortcuts. It has two tabs: `Pinned` shows the menus you pinned, and `All Menu` shows every menu you may open.
3. To pin a menu, hover over it in the sidebar and select the pin icon. It then appears under `Pinned`. To remove it, use the small button in the corner of its card.
4. Read the cards: `Employees`, `Customers`, `Active Projects`, `Total Tickets`, `Active Tickets` and `SLA Compliance`.
5. Under `Recent Tickets`, open a ticket directly, or select `View all` to go to the full list.
6. Some roles also see `Ticket Status Breakdown`, `Ticket Submissions` (the last 30 days) and `Agent Workload` (active tickets per agent). Use them to spot a build-up early.
7. Use `Quick Navigation` for one-click access to pages such as `Tickets`, `My Tasks` and `Reporting`.

## What happens next

Pinned menus are remembered for your account, so they are there the next time you sign in.

## Common questions

**`Pinned` is empty.** You have not pinned anything yet. Pin the menus you use every day.

**A menu I need is not in `All Menu`.** It is not part of your role. Ask your administrator.

**The sidebar looks different from a colleague's.** Menus follow each person's role.
