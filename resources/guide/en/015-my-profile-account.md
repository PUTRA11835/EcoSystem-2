`My Profile` shows your own employee record. You see only your own data, never anyone else's.

## Who uses this

Every employee who has the `My Profile` menu.

## Before you start

- Open it from the menu under your name in the top-right corner.
- Some data can only be changed by HR or an administrator. A section marked `View Only` is meant to be read, not edited.

## Step by step

### Read your profile

![My Profile with the header card and the tabs Basic Data, Address, Identification, Family, Education, Qualification, Contract and Bank Account](images/guide/my-profile.png)

*Screenshot with sample data.*

1. Open `My Profile`.
2. Read the header card. It shows your name, your position, the badges `Active` and your employee type, and your `Employee ID (ECI)`, `Email (Work)`, `Phone`, `Department`, `Division` and `Since Date`.
3. Open the tabs below it one by one: `Basic Data`, `Address`, `Identification`, `Family`, `Education`, `Qualification`, `Contract` and `Bank Account`. The tabs you see depend on your access.
4. Look for the `View Only` mark next to a section title. It tells you that you cannot change that section yourself.

### Ask for a correction

1. Note which field is wrong and what it should be.
2. Send it to HR or your administrator. Do not send identity or bank numbers through chat; give the exact tab and field instead.

### Change your password

Your password is not changed here. Use `Settings` › `Security`, as described in [[sign-in-and-security]].

## What happens next

When HR corrects your data, you see the new value the next time you open the tab.

## Common questions

**A tab is missing.** Your access does not include it. Ask your administrator.

**A field is greyed out.** It is `View Only`. Only HR or an administrator can change it.

**I do not see `My Profile`.** Your role does not include the menu. Ask your administrator.
