This guide explains how to do your work in EcoSystem, step by step and in the right order. It shows only the topics for the features you can use.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in to EcoSystem. Employees, managers, HR, Finance and administrators all see the same guide, but each person sees the topics that match their own access.

## How to use the guide

1. Open `User Guide` from the sidebar, or from the menu under your name in the top-right corner.
2. Choose a chapter and topic in the `Table of Contents` on the left. The page scrolls to it. Chapters and topics are numbered (for example 3.2), and the sub-headings of the topic you are reading appear underneath it. On a phone or a narrow window, open `Table of Contents` at the top first.
3. Use the search box above the table of contents to find a word anywhere in the guide. Select a result to jump to the right place; the words you searched for are highlighted.
4. Press `/` on your keyboard to move straight to the search box.
5. Select `Download PDF` to save the guide you are reading, in the same language, for offline use or printing.

> [!TIP]
> Each topic follows the same pattern: who uses it, what to prepare, the steps, what happens next, and common questions. Skim the headings first, then read the part you need.

## Language

The guide opens in the language you chose in `Settings`. Use the `English` and `Indonesia` switch at the top of the page to change it for this guide at any time. Names of menus, buttons and statuses always stay in English, exactly as they appear on screen, so you can match the text with what you see.

## Why some topics are missing

The guide shows only the topics for menus you have access to. If a feature you expect is not listed, your role does not include that menu yet. See [[access-and-menus]] for what to do.

## The date at the top

`Updated` is the date of the most recent change among the topics you can see. Each topic also shows when it was last updated at the bottom.
