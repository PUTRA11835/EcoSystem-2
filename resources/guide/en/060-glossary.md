Names of menus, buttons and statuses stay in English on screen. This list gives a plain meaning for each term and the Indonesian wording used in everyday speech. The Indonesian guide itself keeps many of these terms in English.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in to EcoSystem.

## Requests and statuses

| Term | Indonesian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Draft` | Draft | Saved but not sent yet; you can still change it |
| `Submitted` | Terkirim | Sent for a decision |
| `Pending` | Menunggu | Waiting for someone to act |
| `Approved` | Disetujui | Accepted by the approver |
| `Rejected` | Ditolak | Declined; the reason is shown |
| `Cancelled` | Dibatalkan | Stopped and no longer active |
| `Approval` | Persetujuan | The decision step on a request |
| `Approver` | Approver (pemberi persetujuan) | The person who decides |
| `Request` | Permintaan / pengajuan | Anything you ask for, such as leave or a purchase |
| `Notification` | Notification | A message in the bell icon about something that needs you |
| `View Only` | Hanya lihat | Data that you can read but not change, for example a locked profile |

## People and HR

| Term | Indonesian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Employee` | Pegawai | A person with a record in the system |
| `Consultant` | Consultant | An external person who works under a contract |
| `External` | External | The section for external consultants |
| `Role` | Role (peran) | A set of access given to a group of users |
| `Menu Access` | Menu Access | The list of menus and actions a role may use |
| `Onboarding` | Onboarding | Completing a new employee's data and verifying it |
| `Offboarding` | Offboarding | The process when someone leaves |
| `Recruitment` | Recruitment | Selecting candidates for open jobs |
| `Offering Letter` | Offering Letter | The offer sent to a chosen candidate |
| `Contract` | Contract (kontrak) | The employment or assignment agreement |
| `Attendance` | Presensi | Daily check-in and check-out records |
| `Check-in` / `Check-out` | Check-in / Check-out | Recording your arrival and departure |
| `Leave & Permit` | Cuti dan izin | Time off requests |
| `Overtime` | Lembur | Work beyond normal hours |
| `KPI` | KPI | Key performance indicators used to assess work |
| `ESS` | ESS | Employee self-service: your own workspace |
| `HR`, `GA` | HR, GA | Human resources and general affairs |

## Money

| Term | Indonesian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Payroll` | Penggajian | The monthly salary calculation |
| `Paystub` | Slip gaji | Your payslip for a period |
| `BPJS` | BPJS | Social security contributions |
| `PPh 21` | PPh 21 | Income tax on employment income |
| `Reimbursement` | Penggantian biaya | A claim for money you spent |
| `Cash Advance` | Cash Advance (uang muka) | Money given in advance for work costs |
| `Cash Advance Report` | Cash Advance Report | Your account of how the advance was spent |
| `Purchase Request` | Purchase Request (permintaan pembelian) | A request to buy something |
| `Purchase Order` | Purchase Order | The order sent after a request is approved |
| `Invoice` | Invoice (tagihan) | A bill for work done |

## Work and service

| Term | Indonesian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Ticket` | Ticket | A support request or task |
| `Ticket Lead` | Ticket lead | The person responsible for a ticket |
| `PIC` | PIC (penanggung jawab) | Person in charge |
| `SLA` | SLA | Service level agreement: the response and resolution times agreed with a customer |
| `Ball Holder` | Ball Holder | Who the ticket is waiting on |
| `Hold` | Hold (ditahan) | A ticket paused for now |
| `Mandays` / `MD` | Mandays | Days of work, used for effort and quotas |

| `IO Number` | Nomor IO | The order number of a project or support contract |
