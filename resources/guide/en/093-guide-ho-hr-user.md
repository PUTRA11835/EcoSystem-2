This guide is for the HO HR User role. It is a small role, and the HR module is still being built.

## Who uses this

Employees with the HO HR User role. Your menus are `Home`, `AI Research`, `HR & General` and `Calendar` › `Events`, plus `Settings` and `User Guide`.

## Before you start

- `HR & General` is still under development. It shows a `Coming Soon` notice for now, so there is nothing to do there yet.
- Do not put personal data of employees into AI Research.

## Step by step

1. Open `Home` after you sign in. See [[home-dashboard]].
2. Open `HR & General` to check whether the module is ready. For now it only shows the `Coming Soon` message.
3. Open `Calendar` › `Events` to see events, and to add one if your role allows it. See [[calendar-timesheet]].
4. Open `Settings` to keep your account safe. See [[sign-in-and-security]].

## What happens next

When the HR module is released, its features appear in `HR & General` and this guide is updated.

## Common questions

**`HR & General` only says `Coming Soon`.** The module is not released yet. Ask your administrator for the schedule.

**I cannot see employee records.** Employee data is managed by roles with the `Master` menus. Ask your administrator if you need access.
