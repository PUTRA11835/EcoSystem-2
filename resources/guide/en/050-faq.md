Short answers to common questions. Each answer points to the topic with the full steps.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in to EcoSystem.

## Common questions

**1. A menu I expected is not in my sidebar. Why?**
Menus follow your role. If your role does not include a menu, it is hidden. Ask your administrator to add the access. See [[access-and-menus]].

**2. I can open a page, but I do not see a button such as `Create`, `Approve` or `Add`.**
Buttons appear only if your role is allowed to do that action. A page can be open for viewing while the action is not. Ask your administrator for the permission.

**3. My profile shows `View Only` and I cannot edit it.**
HR has verified and locked the data. Contact HR; they can unlock the profile and must give a reason when they do. See [[my-profile]] and [[onboarding]].

**4. My request has been `Pending` for days.**
It is waiting for an approver. Check the status of the request, then ask the approver to open `My Approvals`. The approver sees it there with `Waiting since`. See [[statuses-and-approvals]] and [[my-approvals]].

**5. My request was `Rejected`. What now?**
Open the request and read the reason. Correct what is needed and submit again where the form allows it. Otherwise create a new request.

**6. How do I change the language of this guide?**
Use the `English` and `Indonesia` switch at the top of the guide. To change the language of the application, open `Settings` › `Language & Region`. Names of menus, buttons and statuses stay in English in both cases. See [[welcome]].

**7. I checked in with the wrong time, or forgot to check out.**
Use `Request correction` on `My Attendance`. Pick the time with the time picker and add a reason. HR reviews the request, and you can follow it under `My Correction Requests`. If the button shows `Corrections are off`, your company has switched self-service corrections off; contact HR. See [[my-attendance]].

**8. My payslip is not in `Paystub`.**
Payslips appear only after Finance publishes the payroll period, or right after calculation if the automatic option is switched on. If it is late, ask Finance. See [[my-paystub]] and [[workflow-finance]].

**9. I cannot edit a delivery project.**
The project may be closed. A closed project is read-only until someone with the right reopens it with `Reopen Project`. See [[delivery]].

**10. A ticket my customer sent is not in the `Ticket` list.**
New customer tickets first wait in `Ticket Validation` until someone approves them. See [[ticket-validation]].

**11. I forgot my password.**
Click `Forgot password?` on the sign-in page. If you are already signed in, you can change it in `Settings`.

**12. Can I download this guide?**
Yes. Select `Download PDF` on the guide page. It saves the guide in the language you are reading. See [[welcome]].
