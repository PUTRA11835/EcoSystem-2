Use this checklist to see what is expected of you in EcoSystem. The items you can use depend on the menus your role has, so some lines may not apply to you.

## Who uses this

Every employee who signs in. Consultants working through the External section use the same basics, plus their own pages.

## Checklist

### When you start

- Complete your data in `My Profile`. See [[my-profile]]. If HR has verified and locked it, it shows `View Only` and only HR can reopen it.
- Look at your `Dashboard` and the Notification bell. See [[navigating-ecosystem]].
- Set your theme and language in `Settings`.

### Every working day

- Check in and check out in `My Attendance`. See [[my-attendance]].
- If a time is wrong, use `Request correction` on the same page.
- Open the Notification bell for decisions and messages.

### When you need something

| I want to... | Open | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Ask for leave or permission | `My Leave & Permit` | [[my-leave-permit]] |
| Claim overtime | `Overtime` | [[my-overtime]] |
| Claim an expense | `My Reimbursement` | [[my-reimbursement]] |
| Request a purchase | `My Purchase Request` | [[my-purchase-request]] |
| Ask for a cash advance | `My Cash Advance` | [[my-cash-advance]] |
| Ask for a letter | `My Letter Requests` | [[my-letter-requests]] |
| View my payslip | `Paystub` | [[my-paystub]] |
| See my KPI | `My KPI` | [[my-kpi]] |

### Each month

- Check that your attendance, leave and overtime are complete before the payroll period closes.
- Open `Paystub` after payroll is published. Slips appear there once Finance publishes them, or right after calculation if the automatic option is on.

## Step by step: submit a request

1. Open the menu for your request.
2. Create a new request and fill every required field.
3. Add attachments if the form asks for them.
4. Click `Submit`. The request goes to your approver and shows its status.
5. Follow the progress. Your approver decides, and you are notified. See [[statuses-and-approvals]].

> [!TIP]
> If a request is `Rejected`, read the reason, correct what is needed and submit again where the form allows it.

## What happens next

Approved requests flow into HR and Finance processes, such as payroll. Your approver and HR see the same request, so you do not need to send it twice.
